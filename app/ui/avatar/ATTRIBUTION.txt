User-provided Aperture Signs avatar

The bundled GLB was rigged and exported from the user-supplied
`Aperture_Signs_Human_Avatar _001.ccProject.fbx` with Blender 5.2.1. Automatic
weights and localized finger corrections were used; no add-on was installed.
The base-color and normal textures are embedded in the GLB. Some shirt and
underarm creasing may appear in bent-arm poses.

The source asset's author and license were not provided. Verify permission to
redistribute the model before including it in a public app build.
