Aperture Signs generated avatar

The bundled GLB was generated from the MPFB human base mesh using the MPFB
Blender extension. The skin, clothing colors, eyes, and hair cap in this first
version are procedural additions made for Aperture Signs; no external textures
are included.

MPFB is distributed under GPL-3.0-or-later. The MakeHuman Community asset-pack
catalog lists the MakeHuman system assets under CC0, but software and model
asset licenses are separate. Verify the exact license of the bundled MPFB base
mesh before redistributing the generated avatar or a public app build.
